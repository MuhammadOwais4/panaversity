# Prompts Used — Task 2: Gmail Connector

## Prompt 1 (initial connection request)
Connect to my Gmail account and find my most recent email thread that looks like a client
project inquiry. Give me a summary of what the client is asking for.

## Prompt 2 (verification prompt)
Is this email still unread? And did the client mention any specific budget or timeline?

## Prompt 3 (permission check)
What exact permission did you use to access my Gmail — can you read all my emails, only
unread ones, or only specific labels?
